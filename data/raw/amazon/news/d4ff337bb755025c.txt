Amazon facility in Byhalia evacuated following report of gas or chemical leak
BYHALIA, Miss. (WMC) - An Amazon facility in Byhalia was evacuated Tuesday morning following reports of a gas or chemical leak, according to the Marshall County Sheriff’s Office.
Officials confirmed the evacuation took place around 8 a.m. Deputies with the Marshall County Sheriff’s Office are actively investigating the facility to confirm the exact nature and cause of the incident.
Photos from the scene sent to Action News 5 show ambulances staged outside the building.
Action News 5 has a crew heading to the scene to gather more information. This is a developing story and updates will be provided as details become available.
