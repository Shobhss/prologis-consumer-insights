Kuehne+Nagel has entered into a long-term strategic collaboration with its customer, Amazon.
The collaboration is comprehensive in its scope. Building on an already strong relationship, it is designed to support future business opportunities and further strengthen customer services for Amazon and its affiliates.
“We are excited to enter this long-term collaboration with Amazon, with a shared focus on scale, innovation and customer value. Kuehne+Nagel’s global network, digital capabilities and supply chain expertise are well positioned to support Amazon’s evolving business needs, enabling us to accelerate future growth opportunities and create lasting value for our customers. We are setting the foundation for a new level of customer service commitment at a global scale for Amazon,” said Stefan Paul, CEO of Kuehne+Nagel International AG.
The collaboration includes Amazon Web Services (AWS), encompassing the infrastructure lifecycle from construction and equipment deployment to ongoing maintenance, upgrades and expansion projects.
By building on its relationship with Amazon and AWS, Kuehne+Nagel will strengthen supply chain resilience, scalability and operational efficiency, supporting the continued growth of cloud infrastructure globally.
“Building on an already strong relationship, this long-term collaboration positions Kuehne+Nagel to support Amazon’s evolving business needs, including through our Air Logistics network worldwide,” commented a further Kuehne+Nagel spokesperson.
“In particular, we see significant potential to support Amazon Web Services (AWS) with logistics and supply chain services across the infrastructure lifecycle, from construction and equipment deployment to maintenance, upgrades and expansion projects worldwide.”
