Amazon closes Port St. Lucie warehouse for major renovation, hundreds impacted by layoffs
ST. LUCIE COUNTY, Fla. (CBS12) — Amazon’s Port St. Lucie fulfillment center is closing Thursday for a major renovation, leaving hundreds of workers out of a job while others have transferred to different facilities.
When Amazon filed its layoff notice in July, 494 employees were listed as affected. Since then, more than 200 workers have transferred to other Amazon locations, according to information provided to CBS12 News.
Around 300 employees are expected to take severance as the Port St. Lucie facility shuts down.
Local workforce organizations, including CareerSource Research Coast and the Economic Development Council of St. Lucie County, have spent recent months holding job fairs and helping affected workers connect with other employers.
St. Lucie County Commissioner Cathy Townsend said the closure highlights the importance of attracting a wider range of businesses to the county.
“I think we need to be more strategic on the companies we want coming here,” Townsend said. “My focus would be more on small businesses.”

The closure is expected to last until at least 2028.
Amazon plans to invest about $200 million to renovate and expand the facility. The St. Lucie EDC said the work will include advanced robotics and artificial intelligence capabilities, along with an additional 870,000 square feet.
Amazon plans to reopen the facility in the second half of 2028 and hire more than 1,000 employees.
Workers who were employed at the facility before the closure will be eligible for rehire when it reopens, according to the EDC.
Amazon has said the closure is not expected to impact customers because operations will be handled by other facilities during the renovation.
