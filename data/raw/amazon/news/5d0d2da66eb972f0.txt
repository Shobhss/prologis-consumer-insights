Amazon opens 1 million-square-foot Visalia distribution center, creating 600-plus jobs
VISALIA, Calif. (FOX26) — Amazon has opened a new distribution center in Visalia that the company says will speed up deliveries across Central California while creating more than 600 full-time jobs.
The more than 1 million-square-foot facility launched operations on Sunday and is Amazon’s third logistics site in the region.
Amazon said the site is designed to optimize storage and inventory flow, allowing the company to deliver a wider selection of products to customers faster.
The new facility is located at 4001 North Plaza Drive in Visalia.
The Visalia distribution center will function as a regional hub, receiving products directly from vendors and other Amazon facilities and replenishing smaller fulfillment centers that handle final delivery to customers.
Amazon said the Visalia site will handle about 80 percent of inbound volume for those local facilities, reducing delivery distances and enabling more items to qualify for same-day and next-day delivery.
The building uses multi-level material handling equipment and high-density racking systems to maximize storage capacity.
Visalia Mayor Brett Taylor said, “We are excited about the third Amazon facility. Amazon's investment in Visalia is a win for our local economy and for the hardworking families in our community. More than 600 new jobs with competitive pay and benefits from day one – that's real economic impact."
This facility strengthens Visalia's position as a hub for growth in the Central Valley, and we look forward to our continued partnership with Amazon.
Natalie Banke, PR manager for Amazon Field Communications Team, said, “This facility allows us to get products to customers in Central California faster and more efficiently than ever before. Visalia's central location is a significant advantage -- it positions inventory closer to the communities we serve, which means quicker deliveries and a better selection of items available for same-day and next-day shipping."
Amazon said the facility has created more than 600 full-time positions with benefits available from day one, including healthcare, dental and vision coverage. The company also highlighted upskilling opportunities through its Career Choice program, which pre-pays tuition for employees to earn degrees and certifications in high-demand fields.
Amazon said it is still hiring for the Visalia facility. Job listings are updated weekly at amazon.com/localjobs.
People can also receive text alerts about future opportunities by texting JOBEVENT to 31432. Applicants must be 18 or older.
