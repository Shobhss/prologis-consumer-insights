COLONIE — A subcontractor for Amazon is closing its operation at a Colonie distribution hub used by the online retailer.
ThreeC Logistics says it plans to end operations at 1 Mustang Drive on Nov. 11.

The decision will affect 78 workers, according to a Worker Adjustment and Retraining Notification filed with the state Labor Department.
ThreeC Logistics is one of several contractors, known as delivery service partners, that handle deliveries out of Amazon’s warehouse and distribution hub, or delivery station, in Colonie, just off Route 9.

U.S. Department of Transportation records indicate that ThreeC Logistics is located in Suffern, Rockland County.
The Times Union reached out to company management on Wednesday by phone. The person who answered hung up after a reporter asked why the business was closing or whether it had lost its contract with Amazon.
The state was notified of the closure in August and published the WARN notice on Wednesday. The state-mandated notices are intended to give workers forewarning about job cuts at companies that employ 25 or more people.

Amazon has 4,400 delivery service partners, or DSPs, that it works with. The company is always recruiting people to start new DSP companies from scratch. The startup costs are as low as $10,000, the company suggests on its website. These DSPs employ 390,000 people in 20 countries, Amazon says.
The Times Union has reached out to Amazon for comment but has yet to hear back.
