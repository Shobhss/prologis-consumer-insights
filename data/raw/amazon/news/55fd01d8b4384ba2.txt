Amazon is set to add more industrial space in the Richmond region, this time in New Kent.
The e-commerce giant plans to build a distribution center of more than 1 million square feet at the exit 211 interchange along I-64, county leaders announced on Tuesday.
The project would take shape on a currently vacant and forested 170-acre property at 7770 City Center Court, adjacent to the existing AutoZone warehouse facility in the New Kent City Center industrial park in Providence Forge.
The project site was purchased recently by Amazon for an undisclosed amount from Matan Companies, per the county release. Online property records did not yet reflect the sale as of Tuesday afternoon. An entity tied to Matan purchased the property in 2024 for $15.1 million, per property records.
The county’s announcement said Amazon plans to begin construction this month, with a tentative completion in late 2027. The distribution facility would serve the company’s other fulfillment centers.
“New Kent County staff, the Economic Development Authority and the Board of Supervisors have worked diligently over the past several years to draw major players, such as AutoZone, Target, and now Amazon, to our community. This is another major win for New Kent County business,” New Kent County Board of Supervisors Chair Ron Stiers said in a statement.
The release states that the total investment amount and number of jobs created from the project are still to be determined. The county also noted that it did not provide economic development incentives related to the Amazon project.
Called “Project Cardinal” in final project site plans submitted in April, Amazon’s plans consists of one approximately 1.05 million-square-foot building, along with parking areas, curbing, water and sewer systems. Access to the site would be provided from an existing cul-de-sac along City Center Court at the northeast corner of the site.
Amazon is directly mentioned in the site plan.
The one-story building would be 75 feet tall and would include 45,000 square feet of office space. The facility would be on public water and sewer.
Plans list a total of 551 passenger vehicle parking spaces and a combined total of 349 dock and trailer spaces for trucks.
Kimley-Horn and Associates is the civil engineer listed in project documents.
The New Kent announcement comes just over a year after Amazon broke ground on a 3.1-million square-foot robotics fulfillment center on a 105-acre site at 2022 Ashland Road in the Rockville area of Goochland.
That center is set to open its doors next year and will be the largest Amazon facility in the greater Richmond area; the company previously opened a 2.7-million-square-foot robotics fulfillment center near Richmond Raceway in March of 2024.
The company is also said to be the tenant of a 188,000-square-foot warehouse in Meadowville Technology Park in Chesterfield local development firm Lingerfelt sold last year for $31.3 million.
